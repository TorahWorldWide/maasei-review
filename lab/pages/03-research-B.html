מ-013 · אתר הארגון (אנגלית), לכידה 30.6.1998 — "History": התאגדה כמלכ"ר ב-1976; על שם שרה אם האב; פיקדון קטן מוחזר להחזרת הפריט · דרגה ג - ציטוט: »It all began in the 1970s. A young Jerusalem high school teacher with a growing family needed to borrow a vaporizer from a neighbor for a sick child. … The teacher, Uri Lupolianski, saw there was a real need for this kind of help. Around that time, his father, the late Jacob Lupolianski, retired and sold his small shop. He offered to use the money to help. And so, in 1976, the Yad Sarah Organization was incorporated as a non-profit organization. It was named for Jacob's mother, Sarah, who had perished in the Holocaust. The work "Yad," which literally means "hand," is also used to mean a remembrance or a memorial. Space was offered near a local hospital, a stock of equipment was purchased, and Yad Sarah was a reality. … In order to keep track of the inventory, a small deposit was asked for, refundable when the item was returned. … Today more than 250,000 Israelis use Yad Sarah each year. And in 1994, only 18 years after it was founded, Yad Sarah received the highest award given by the State of Israel: The Israel Prize« - לינק: https://web.archive.org/web/19980630073552/http://www.yadsarah.org.il:80/About/eng_history.html · סנפשוט web.archive.org_949542bee4 · נכון ל-1998. - סתירה פנימית קטנה בעדות העצמית: הגרסה העברית (מ-012) אומרת "שכן שילדו חלה"; הגרסה האנגלית: "for a sick child" של המורה עצמו? — לא, גם כאן "needed to borrow a vaporizer from a neighbor for a sick child" (לא נאמר של מי). התמליל (מ-040) מדבר על ילדי השכונה ועל "התינוק". לפרק הסתירות: מי היה הילד.
