A young Jerusalem high school teacher with a growing family needed to borrow a vaporizer from a neighbor for a sick child. … The teacher, Uri Lupolianski, saw there was a real need for this kind of help. Around that time, his father, the late Jacob Lupolianski, retired and sold his small shop. He offered to use the money to help. And so, in 1976, the Yad Sarah Organization was incorporated as a non-profit organization. It was named for Jacob's mother, Sarah, who had perished in the Holocaust.
